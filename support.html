Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get more name searches?
Free searches reset daily. You can also watch a short ad for an extra search, or upgrade to Premium for unlimited searches from the Settings tab.

How do I restore my Premium purchase?
Open Settings > Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple. Open Settings > Manage Subscription in the app, or go to Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions on your iPhone.

Is my data private?
Yes. See our Privacy Policy for details.
