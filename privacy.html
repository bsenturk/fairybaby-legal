Privacy Policy

Last updated July 11, 2026

This policy explains what information FairyBaby ("the app", "we", "us") collects, why, and how it's handled. FairyBaby is a baby name discovery app that suggests names using AI-generated meaning, origin, and numerology insights.

FairyBaby does not require an account, does not sell your personal data, and only collects what's needed to run the app and its free, ad-supported tier.

Information You Provide

When you use the name wizard, you may enter preferences (gender, traits, a numerology number, a cultural origin) and, optionally, parent names used to personalize style matching. These inputs are sent to our backend, which uses OpenAI to generate name suggestions in your app's language. This data is used to generate your results and is not linked to your identity.

Information Stored on Your Device

Names you favorite are saved locally on your device only. We don't have a server-side account system, so your favorites list is never uploaded to us and is removed if you delete the app.

Advertising & Tracking

FairyBaby shows ads (including rewarded ads for extra searches) through Google AdMob. On iOS, an App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt asks your permission before any cross-app tracking identifier is used. If you decline, you'll still see ads, just less personalized. A consent form (via Google's User Messaging Platform) is shown where required by law (e.g. GDPR in the EEA/UK) so you can control ad personalization. You can change your tracking permission anytime in your device's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Purchases & Subscriptions

Premium subscriptions are processed by Apple and managed via RevenueCat, which we use to verify and sync your purchase status across devices. RevenueCat receives an anonymous purchase/device identifier, never your payment details, which are handled entirely by Apple.

Third-Party Services

Google AdMob / UMP (ad serving and consent management), RevenueCat (subscription management), OpenAI via our backend (generates name meaning, origin, and numerology text), and Apple (App Store purchases). Each provider processes data under their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

FairyBaby is intended for use by parents and expecting parents, not children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your Choices

You can decline App Tracking Transparency at any time in iOS Settings, delete the app to remove all locally stored favorites and preferences, or contact us to ask what data we hold or to request deletion of any data associated with your purchase records.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date above.
